THE LAWS OF KINGDOM

How to play

The shape of a round

  1. ❖DRAWTake each card from the pile you choose
  2. ✎PLANPlace in secret, and take it back if you think better of it
  3. ⚔COMMITBoth plans are revealed at the same moment
  4. ✹CLASHBallistas fire, then every lane west to east
  5. ≋HARVESTBuildings and surviving workers pay out

The laws

01

Draw from two piles

Your banner splits into structures and units when battle begins. You are dealt three structures and two units to open; after that you choose which pile each draw comes from. A round opens on the draw step and planning stays locked until you have spent it. Two draws a round, plus one for each University standing. A hand holds ten — draws into a full hand are forfeited, and the card stays on its pile.

02

Plan in secret

Buildings go in your back row, units in the lane in front of them. You may build over an occupied slot; the old card is sacrificed to the discard when you commit. Click a card you placed this round to take it back and be refunded in full. Nothing either ruler places is visible to the other until both commit.

03

The clash, in order

Ballistas fire first, before any unit moves. Then each lane resolves west to east. A Fire Sapper burns out and takes the opposing unit with it. Otherwise the higher power wins and the loser falls; equal power destroys both. A unit facing no one strikes the enemy ruler for its power — unless it is a Battering Ram, which breaks the building behind instead. Power is counted before the first blow, so adjacency bonuses are locked in as the lanes meet.

04

Feed the realm

Camps cost nothing and harvest one. The Mill, Sawmill and Forge harvest two and are each bought with two of the very resource they make, so a realm ramps on its own harvest. Workers harvest only after surviving a full round, then every second round. Gold stands in for any missing food, wood or metal.

05

Shape your banner

A banner is exactly twenty cards with at most four copies of any design, built from a collection of twenty designs. Win a battle and the week’s pack offers a new design — taking it means sacrificing one you already hold, so the collection stays at twenty and every banner swaps its copies over automatically.

06

The weekly decree

One decree rules the whole week, and it binds both rulers equally. A decree can change what you draw, what your buildings yield, or how much of the field you may use at all. They turn on the calendar, so read the Hall before you rebuild.
