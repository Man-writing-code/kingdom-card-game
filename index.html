THE LAWS OF KINGDOM

How to play

01

Plan in secret

Each round, play buildings into your back row and units into the lane before them. You may replace an occupied slot. Your rival’s choices remain hidden until you commit.

02

Clash by lane

Units in opposing lanes clash at the end of the round. The lower-power unit falls; equal power destroys both. Unblocked units strike the enemy ruler.

03

Grow your realm

Basic camps produce one resource; Mills, Forges, and Sawmills produce two but require a larger investment. Workers produce after surviving a full round, then every second round. Gold may cover missing resources.

04

Grow your hand

Normally, begin with five cards and keep anything unplayed. Each new round draws two cards. Town Halls recruit a random worker; Universities add another draw. Weekly decrees may change the base hand or draw.
